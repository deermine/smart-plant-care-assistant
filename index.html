Designed to optimize indoor household plant care using real-time soil moisture, temperature, and sun exposure data to prevent overwatering and environmental stress.

Quick Test Scenarios:

The Problem

Indoor plants frequently die from misinterpreting water or sunlight needs. Raw numbers (like lux or raw moisture %) are confusing for everyday owners to decode manually.

Our AI Solution

A beginner-friendly AI prototype that evaluates environmental threshold logic and converts raw telemetry into clear visual badges and dynamic plant care recommendations.
